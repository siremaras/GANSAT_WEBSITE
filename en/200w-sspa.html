The GANSAT-200WKU delivers +53.2 dBm of Ku-Band RF output for teleport hubs, DTH broadcast uplink stations, and large VSAT hubs, providing high link margin for demanding applications. Its 220V AC GaN solid-state design operates reliably from -40°C to +55°C in harsh outdoor environments.

At 200W output, GaN technology achieves this performance with only 1170W of AC input — a significant efficiency advantage over legacy TWTA designs.
